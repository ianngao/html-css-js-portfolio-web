    August 2023 - May 2024

    Integrating Online Learning and Connectivity Maintenance for Communication-Aware Multi-Robot Coordination

    This research introduces a novel **Data-driven Connectivity Maintenance (DCM)** framework for multi-robot systems. It leverages **Gaussian Processes (GP)** and **Control Barrier Functions (CBF)** to ensure robots maintain high-quality communication without predefined distance constraints. The approach enables real-time **RSSI-based connectivity modeling**, allowing robot teams to adaptively optimize their motion while staying connected. Theoretical proofs and simulations with up to 20 robots demonstrate its effectiveness in **maintaining global connectivity**.
